# Ocelle — preview videos

These are recordings of the **real interface**: the built `site/` served locally, driven by Playwright, captured frame by frame through the Chrome DevTools screencast. Nothing is mocked or edited after capture. The only additions are the brand band, the captions burned in with ffmpeg/libass, a tap indicator (a white ring where the "finger" taps), and the end card. Fonts are the brand's own (Lexend and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, served locally from @fontsource). External requests were blocked.

Encoding: H.264 (libx264, `-preset veryfast -crf 22`), yuv420p, 30 fps, `+faststart`, no audio.
- 9:16 = 1080×1920: phone layout (540×800 CSS at 2×) under a 320 px band holding the logo and captions.
- 16:9 = 1920×1080: desktop layout (1280×720 CSS at 1.2×) framed on the night background, with captions in the bottom band.

## Scenes
1. **Cas vedette / Featured case** (`#cas-vedette`). The page opens, then holds about 3 s on the fundus photo (Observe). Tap « Je suis prêt à répondre », tap the right answer (Réponds), and the decisive-sign rings animate on the cherry-red spot, with a numbered legend (Révèle). The page then scrolls to "why each other choice is wrong" and the decisive-sign verdict. End card: logo, tagline and the link `https://impeccasper-rgb.github.io/ophtalmo-academie/?lang=fr#cas-vedette` (or `?lang=en`).
   Captions: « Tu vois le signe ? » → « Observe. Réponds. Révèle. » → « Le signe décisif s’allume : la tache rouge cerise » → « Puis pourquoi chaque autre choix est faux » (EN: "Can you spot the sign?" / "Observe. Answer. Reveal." / "The decisive sign lights up: the cherry-red spot" / "Then why each other choice is wrong").
2. **Examens: simulateur A-scan** (`#inv-ascan`). Drag caliper 1 onto the corneal spike and caliper 2 onto the retinal spike, then « Vérifier ma mesure »: 23.5 mm, correct. Switch to « Œil long » and move caliper 2 to 26.5 mm: correct, axial myopia. End card links to `#inv-ascan`.
3. **Image du jour + série / Image of the day + streak** (home card, then `#image-du-jour`). Tap the day's card, look, flip, rate yourself (« Je savais »). The streak goes to **7 days in a row**. End card links to `#image-du-jour`.
   *Demo data:* a 6-day streak was pre-loaded in localStorage so the counter reads 7. The image of the day was pinned to OPH-059 for the recording (normally it is picked by date).
4. **Social variant**: scene 1 on the OCT schematic `p-oct-9` (captions: « Le signe décisif s’allume : du liquide sous la rétine »). It links to the featured case, because a link is not the photo.

## Notes
- The App Store accepts 1080×1920 previews for some iPhone display classes. For 6.9" / 6.5" slots, use the simulator recordings from the iOS workflow, or re-run with that size (the `FORMATS` table in the recorder script).
- To regenerate: build the site (`python3 tools/build_web.py`) and serve `site/` on 127.0.0.1:8766. A reference copy of the recorder scripts is in `videos/recorder/` (see NOTES.md there: fonts and paths).
